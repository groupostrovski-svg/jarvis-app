Jarvis

A personal voice assistant for a single user — its owner. It runs on the owner's own PC and is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

  • Listens to the owner's voice commands on the owner's computer and answers by voice.
  • Reads and, only when the owner asks, updates the owner's Google Calendar.
  • Reads the owner's Gmail only to answer the owner's own voice requests.
  • Controls the owner's smart home devices and apps from an allowed list.

Who can use it

Only the owner. There are no user accounts, sign-ups or other users.
